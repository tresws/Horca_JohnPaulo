# My Favorite Movie

## Favorite Movie: World War Z

World War Z is my favorite movie because it is full of action, suspense, and exciting scenes. It is about a zombie outbreak that spreads around the world.

### Why I Like It
- The story is exciting and interesting.
- The zombie scenes are scary.
- The movie has a lot of action and adventure.
- It shows how people fight to survive during a zombie outbreak.

**My Favorite Actor:** Brad Pitt

**My Favorite Part:** When Gerry Lane discovers how to avoid being attacked by zombies.

*I recommend this movie to people who enjoy action, horror, and zombie movies.*
